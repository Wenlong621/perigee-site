Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Operator: Wenlong Zhang (individual developer)

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Perigee (also shown as Perigee Tasks / 近界; iOS Bundle ID app.perigee.ios) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our iOS app, desktop app, and related cloud sync services.

We build Perigee as a personal productivity tool. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your task content for advertising.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Perigee, we may process:

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers, and sign-in session data (including Sign in with Apple when enabled).
  • Task and project data: titles, descriptions, statuses, priorities, due dates, repeat settings, subtasks, completion notes/history, archive state, and related timestamps.
  • Cover images: images you upload or generate for tasks, and associated metadata (for example, whether a cover was uploaded or AI-generated).
  • Technical / service data: approximate diagnostics needed to operate sync and security (for example, app version, device type at a high level, timestamps, and error logs processed by our infrastructure providers).

You may use local/offline features without cloud sync. Cloud features require an account.

3. How we use information

  • Provide core task management features.
  • Sync your data across devices you sign into with the same account.
  • Authenticate you and protect account access.
  • Store and display task cover images.
  • Improve reliability, troubleshoot issues, and meet legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

We use service providers to operate Perigee:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, realtime sync). Task data, account data, and cover files needed for cloud sync are processed on Supabase infrastructure. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Apple for App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple (when used), push delivery (when enabled), and related platform services.
  • AI cover generation providers (currently including Pollinations or similar image-generation endpoints you choose to use in-app). If you generate an AI cover, the prompt/title text and resulting image request are sent to that provider to create the image. Do not include sensitive personal data in prompts or task titles used for generation.

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services to us and are expected to protect it under their own policies and applicable law.

5. Data storage, transfers, and retention

  • Local device storage may keep a copy of your tasks for offline use.
  • Cloud-synced data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and may be processed in the regions offered by that provider.
  • We retain account and synced content while your account remains active.
  • If you delete your account, we delete or irreversibly de-identify associated cloud account data and synced content within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for security/fraud prevention.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update your tasks directly in the app.
  • Sign out to stop cloud sync on a device.
  • Request account deletion in the app (Settings) once the feature is available, or contact us using the email below.
  • Depending on your location (for example under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

7. Children

Perigee is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards provided by our platform and backend providers (including encrypted transport via HTTPS and access controls such as row-level security for user data). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of Perigee after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

  • 技术与服务数据：为保障同步与安全所需的有限诊断信息（如应用版本、粗粒度设备类型、时间戳、错误日志等，经由基础设施服务商处理）。

  • 存储与展示任务头图；
  • 提升稳定性、排查问题，并履行法律义务。

4. 第三方服务
